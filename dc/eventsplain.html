Introducing the IPRAM Lab for Tokenized Regulated Asset Markets

The tokenisation of regulated real-world assets has led to multiple institutional implementations of the same financial instruments, each operating on its own infrastructure with few shared patterns for how those infrastructures interoperate. As a result, tokenised assets remain confined to the environment they were issued in, accessible only to counterparties already inside the same institutional perimeter.

The IPRAM Lab ([https://github.com/LFDT-IPRAM](https://github.com/LFDT-IPRAM)) develops open implementation patterns for the interoperation of tokenised real-world regulated financial assets and the markets they participate in.
