Friday, November 20 · 5:30–6:30 a.m. Pacific / 13:30–14:30 UTC · Live Zoom session hosted by Rajat Dandekar on Maven. A recording will be available afterward.
You click Send. A cluster of GPUs has to turn a trillion parameters into your next token. Where do the weights fit, what has to move, and what happens when thousands of people are waiting for an answer at once?

* **Split the work across the cluster.** Compare tensor, expert, and pipeline parallelism; see why you might run prefill and decode on separate GPUs; and learn where FP4 and speculative decoding fit.

**Abstract:** Amazon Bedrock AgentCore gives agents secure, isolated compute, managed identity, memory and access to Bedrock models. Agents that run multi-step tasks over minutes or hours depend on external models, tools and APIs, where timeouts, rate limits, errors and redeployments are a normal part of operating at scale. These agents require durable execution. This session adds Diagrid Catalyst as the agentic durable execution layer on AgentCore, so agent runs complete reliably and every step is verifiable.

With verifiable execution, every step is cryptographically signed and traceable to the agent that ran it, producing a tamper-evident record that auditors and risk teams can check. You keep your existing Strands, LangGraph or Mastra code, run on AgentCore and Bedrock, and add one Diagrid package.
