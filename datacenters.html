Method and limits

Data centers are represented as one flat JSON array. The inventory combines government-verified state and permitted facilities, operator-documented commercial facilities, and named proposals. Each record says what kind of evidence establishes its status. Missing energy, UPS, employment, investment, or tax-benefit values remain unknown rather than becoming zero.

Public sentiment is an evidence rating, not opinion polling: -2 means strong, sustained, site-specific opposition; -1 documented opposition; 0 mixed or unclear evidence; +1/+2 documented or strong support. A facility receives no score when the record is insufficient. Formal hearings, enacted restrictions, petitions, organizational letters, and attributable reporting outweigh unsourced social-media comments.

Power-plant capacity and locations come from final 2024 Form EIA-860 records. Production is annual net generation reported in final 2024 Form EIA-923 records. The residential price is EIA’s statewide annual average, not a household quote.

Backup-generator megawatts describe emergency nameplate capacity. They must never be presented as data-center grid demand, consumption, or production.
