# Claude Touches Grass 🌲🥾💻

Close the laptop—but don’t stop coding.
Join Coffee & Code Philadelphia for a hardcore outdoor coding session where developers, founders, researchers, and AI builders take Claude out of the terminal and onto the trail.
Bring your Apple Vision Pro, connect to Claude Code through remote control on your phone, or pack whatever portable setup lets you ship from the wilderness. We’ll hike, find a scenic place to set up, and spend focused time building together surrounded by actual trees instead of desktop wallpaper.
This is not a screen-free wellness retreat. There will be code.

### What to expect

* A social group hike with Philadelphia’s tech community
* Dedicated outdoor coding and building time
* Claude Code sessions controlled remotely from your phone
* Apple Vision Pro and other unconventional coding setups
* Conversations about AI, startups, open source, and whatever you’re building
* Optional food or coffee with the group afterward

Bring a project you want to advance, a difficult bug you want to defeat, or a completely unreasonable idea you want to prototype in the woods.
All hiking and coding experience levels are welcome. We’ll share the route, distance, difficulty, parking information, and weather updates before the event.

### What to bring

* A phone with Claude remote control configured
* Apple Vision Pro, laptop, tablet, or another portable setup
* A charged power bank
* Mobile hotspot or cellular data
* Comfortable hiking shoes
* Water and snacks
* Weather-appropriate clothing
* Sunscreen or bug spray as needed
* Your fully online, artificially hosted neural network

Friends are welcome—even the ones who don’t know what Claude is.
For everyone’s safety, save the immersive coding for our designated stops. Do not wear a Vision Pro or stare at your phone while actively hiking.
Come touch grass, summon Claude, and ship something in the wilderness. 🌿

Before the hype. Before the buzzwords. Before “AI-powered everything.”

An open, welcoming space for coders, builders, designers, and curious minds.

* Organic conversations
