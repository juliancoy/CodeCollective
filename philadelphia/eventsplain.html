(CS)²AI Online™: Security Lifecycles of Connected Power Infrastructure

[https://events.zoom.us/ev/Akgd1vdEBM3_-dVIIC984km2rdNba4A7BSm8oQ3Acj8AMaAqEuiG~AgGQltEj_HyS7hzBVFo65QjDQHd2E-V1ve9fe5tBu5wSeD96KYTH_bDG5GJkbKM7mTSe4ZhY8-zItF2ZDowp-8QxwA](https://events.zoom.us/ev/Akgd1vdEBM3_-dVIIC984km2rdNba4A7BSm8oQ3Acj8AMaAqEuiG~AgGQltEj_HyS7hzBVFo65QjDQHd2E-V1ve9fe5tBu5wSeD96KYTH_bDG5GJkbKM7mTSe4ZhY8-zItF2ZDowp-8QxwA)

The power industry has largely solved the question of whether critical power assets should be connected. UPS systems, PDUs, and power management platforms are now routinely integrated with cloud services, remote support, and operational analytics. The harder question is whether these assets are deployed in a way that justifies the trust being placed in them. Even when products are Secure by Design and Secure by Default, commissioning and integration decisions often determine the real cybersecurity outcome. Yet these activities are frequently led by teams focused on operational performance and uptime rather than cyber risk. This session examines why commissioning may be the most important security event in a power asset's lifecycle and outlines practical approaches for establishing security from day one through secure commissioning, secure-by-deployment principles, and lifecycle risk management.

Join our ICS/OT Cybersecurity star Mike Holcomb and Power specialist Saltanat Mashirova for this in-depth talk, and be sure to bring your questions for her!

### A Blockspace Community × Coffee&Code Collaboration

Before the hype. Before the buzzwords. Before “AI-powered everything.”

No panels. No pitches. No pressure.
Just good people, good coffee, and focused time to build, learn, and connect.

## What this meetup is

An open, welcoming space for coders, builders, designers, and curious minds.
Bring your own project, homework, startup, side quest, experiment, or idea. Work at your own pace, meet other builders, ask questions, exchange ideas, or simply enjoy being productive around like-minded people.

* Organic conversations
* Spontaneous debugging
* Knowledge sharing
* Genuine community

❌ A lecture
❌ A sales event
❌ A recruiting trap
❌ A 90-minute presentation

1. Show up
2. Grab coffee ☕

4. Work at your own pace
5. Chat when you want and focus when you need
6. Leave having made progress—or at least a new friend

**Securing Your Data for AI**
**Session Description**
AI changes what enterprise data does. Beyond reporting and analytics, data now directly shapes model behavior, retrieval, recommendations, automation, and agent actions — making data governance a security concern. This session uses LLM04 2026: Data and Model Poisoning as an industry reference point to examine how poisoning risk enters through untrusted sources, manipulated content, stale knowledge, unsafe feedback loops, and AI-accessible data that is treated as more authoritative than it should be. The session will connect these risks to the governance controls required to secure data for AI systems.
**Additional Topics**

* How AI expands the enterprise data attack surface
* How poisoned or manipulated content affects RAG retrieval and recommendations
* How compromised fine-tuning sets and feedback loops degrade model behavior
* Source trust, provenance, and authority for AI-accessible data
* Ingestion controls and lifecycle management for AI pipelines
* Vulnerability identification and testing specific to AI data flows
* Mapping LLM04 2026 to practical data governance decisions
