# 🤖 AI Agent Hackathon

## Two Days Two Locations One Hackathon

Join Coffee & Code Philadelphia for a hands-on, in-person hackathon focused on building, experimenting with, and shipping working AI-agent projects.
The main Hackathon Build Day takes place on September 20 at Pennovation Center. Projects, winners, and members of Philadelphia’s AI community will come together again on September 22 for an expanded Awards & Community Night at Cesium.
Come with a team, meet collaborators at the event, or build independently. You do not need to arrive with a finished project idea.

## 👤 Who Should Attend

This event is for developers, engineers, technical builders, founders, researchers, students, and anyone interested in experimenting with AI-agent systems.
Whether you already work with agent frameworks or are just beginning to explore them, the goal is to test ideas, learn from other builders, and leave with something working.
You may participate:

* With an existing team
* By finding collaborators at the event
* As an independent builder
* Without a finished project idea

## 🧠 What You Can Build

Projects may explore:

* Coding agents
* Multi-agent systems
* Tool use and automation
* Agent memory and context
* Evaluation and reliability
* AI safety and security
* Open-source agent frameworks
* Real-world agent applications
* Sponsor challenge tracks
* The Open Track and Bring Your Own Project

## 📦 Submission Requirements

To be eligible for judging, each team or solo builder must submit:

* A working prototype that meaningfully uses AI agents or agentic workflows
* A description of the problem, project, and use of AI agents
* A source-code repository
* A demo video of three minutes or less
* A list of the models, frameworks, APIs, tools, and datasets used
* A complete team-member list
* Setup or reproduction instructions
* A clear explanation of what was created or significantly extended during the hackathon

Projects should be substantially built during the hackathon. Existing libraries, APIs, models, frameworks, boilerplate, and general-purpose tools may be used.
A polished production application is not required. Judges should be able to understand the idea, see a functioning prototype, and evaluate how AI agents are used.
